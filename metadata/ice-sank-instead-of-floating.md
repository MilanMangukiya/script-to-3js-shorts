Ice floats because water does something rare. This 3D thought experiment changes that one rule, lets ice sink, and follows the consequences from a kitchen glass to a frozen lake and the poles.

• Water expands by about 9% as it freezes into an open crystal, so ice is lighter than the liquid.
• That floating lid works like a blanket: beneath it, lakes stay liquid all winter and life waits out the cold.
• If ice sank, every crystal that formed at the surface would drop to the bottom while the bare water above kept freezing.
• Winter after winter, cold lakes could freeze from the bottom up, squeezing fish into a shrinking layer of water.
• No skating, ice roads or ice fishing. No floating sea ice or icebergs. Emperor penguins would lose the ice where they raise their chicks.
• One upside: water would shrink as it froze, so frozen pipes would never burst.

Scope: only the density of ice is changed. Bottom-up freezing and wildlife outcomes are labelled illustrative in the film; they are possibilities, not predictions, and no claim is made about the long-term global climate.
