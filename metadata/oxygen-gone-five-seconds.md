What would really happen if all the oxygen in the air vanished for five seconds, then came back? This 3D thought experiment follows the missing oxygen from a candle flame to orbit and home again.

• Oxygen is about a fifth of the air, so air pressure would drop by about a fifth. Sea level would feel like roughly 2 km of altitude.
• Your ears would pop, but you wouldn't suffocate: your blood already carries enough oxygen for a few seconds.
• Every flame that feeds on air would go out. Jet engines would flame out and airliners would sag in the thinner air.
• Fish would hardly notice, because the oxygen dissolved in the sea stays put.
• When the oxygen returns, hot embers can flare back to life, but power plants that lost their flames could stay dark for hours.
