## Accuracy note

Assumes Earth is placed into a stable circular 0.90 AU orbit with present solar luminosity and initial atmosphere/oceans. The 23.5% flux increase, 11.1% diameter increase and ~312-day year are derived. Climate, regional drying and heat stress are illustrative model-dependent responses; no dated transition or universal wet-bulb survival threshold is predicted. Cloud opacity is a visual illustration, not a climate-model output. UV chemistry and orbital sizes are labeled schematics; long-term ocean loss is geological, not immediate. The final outward move returns to present conditions for the visual loop.
